How Nyx works

Every team gets a power rating from season-to-date stats, weighted by what actually predicts winning: net EPA per play (25%), sack differential (25%), turnover differential (15%), net passing yards (15%), 3rd-down rate (10%) and rushing yards (10%). Ratings convert to a win probability, a fair moneyline, and a model spread. Starting-QB injuries adjust ratings (Out ≈ 3 points). Projected scores split the market total by the model spread.

The model is graded every Tuesday after MNF — record, Brier score, and flag hit rate are published whether it won or lost. Weights only change when several weeks of evidence say so.

Player props

In development. Nyx is a team-rating model today. A player-level projection model (usage rates, matchup splits, hit rates vs the actual line) is phase two — we'd rather publish nothing than publish a guess dressed up as a model.
